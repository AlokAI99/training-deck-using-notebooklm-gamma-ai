# training-deck-using-notebooklm-gamma-ai
AI-powered training deck creation using NotebookLM, Gamma AI, Napkin AI, and Canva to automate research, summarization, infographic creation, and presentation design.







## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates how AI tools can automate the creation of a training deck for new Marketing Interns.

By combining NotebookLM, Gamma AI, Napkin AI, and Canva, the workflow transforms educational resources into structured summaries, informative infographics, and professional training presentations.

The primary objective is to reduce repetitive manual work while maintaining accuracy, clarity, and visual consistency through human review.

## 🎯 Use Case

Create an engaging training deck for new Marketing Interns by automating:

- Research and information gathering
- YouTube video summarization
- Training content organization
- Infographic creation
- Presentation generation
- Slide design and visual polishing
- Final review and export

## 🧠 Mindset & Thought Process

The workflow begins by identifying repetitive and time-consuming tasks that can be assisted by AI.

The process follows three principles:

1. **Automate:** Use AI tools to accelerate research, summarization, visual creation, and presentation assembly.
2. **Verify:** Check AI-generated content against the original sources.
3. **Human in the Loop:** Keep human judgment involved in important decisions to maintain accuracy and quality.

AI supports the workflow, but the final content and presentation must be reviewed by a human.

## 🏗️ Solution Design

The solution consists of two main components.

### Component 1: Research and Summarization

Use NotebookLM to analyze supported source materials and create structured summaries from selected YouTube videos and other relevant resources.

Use Napkin AI to create infographics, Gamma AI to generate the initial presentation, and Canva to refine the final design.

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Implementation

### Step 1: Summarize YouTube Videos Using NotebookLM

1. Open NotebookLM and sign in.
2. Create a new notebook for Marketing Intern Training.
3. Add relevant YouTube video links using the supported source options.
4. Confirm that the sources have been processed successfully.
5. Generate a structured summary using the prompt below.

**Prompt:**

> Summarize these videos for Marketing Analyst Training for new Marketing Interns. Organize the content into logical sections. Include key concepts, important terminology, practical examples, marketing strategies, actionable insights, and key takeaways. Avoid repetition and preserve important context. Keep the language clear, professional, and suitable for beginners. Where possible, identify the supporting source for important claims.

6. Review the summary and verify important information against the original sources.

**Output:** Structured training content ready for visual transformation.

### Step 2: Generate Infographics Using Napkin AI

1. Open Napkin AI.
2. Copy the relevant sections from the approved NotebookLM summary.
3. Paste the content into Napkin AI.
4. Generate suitable visual explanations using the available controls.
5. Select appropriate formats, such as process flows, comparisons, and concept diagrams.
6. Refine the visuals and check labels, terminology, and relationships.
7. Export or copy the approved infographics for the presentation.

**Output:** Clear and engaging infographics.

### Step 3: Create a Presentation Using Gamma AI

1. Open Gamma AI.
2. Start a new presentation.
3. Choose the text-based or paste-content creation option, if available.
4. Paste the structured NotebookLM summary.
5. Configure the presentation for new Marketing Interns.
6. Choose concise slide text and a professional theme.
7. Generate the presentation.
8. Review the slide sequence and remove unnecessary repetition.
9. Replace irrelevant image placeholders and refine the content.

**Suggested presentation instructions:**

> Create a professional training presentation for new Marketing Interns. Use concise text, clear headings, practical examples, logical section transitions, and a consistent visual style. Explain key marketing concepts in beginner-friendly language. Include actionable takeaways and avoid unsupported claims.

**Output:** An initial AI-generated training deck.

### Step 4: Polish the Presentation Using Canva

1. Open Canva.
2. Import the presentation or use a supported file format to continue editing.
3. Choose a consistent theme, color palette, and font combination.
4. Improve slide alignment, spacing, hierarchy, and readability.
5. Add approved Napkin AI infographics and relevant visuals.
6. Standardize headings, footers, icons, and slide layouts.
7. Remove unnecessary decorative elements.
8. Export the reviewed presentation in a supported PDF or PPTX format.

**Output:** A polished and visually consistent training deck.

### Step 5: Human Review and Quality Assurance

Before publishing the final presentation, verify:

- **Accuracy:** Check important facts against the original sources.
- **Completeness:** Confirm that all essential training topics are covered.
- **Clarity:** Ensure the content is understandable for beginners.
- **Visual Quality:** Check image relevance, contrast, spacing, and readability.
- **Consistency:** Use standardized fonts, headings, colors, and terminology.
- **Attribution:** Credit source creators and materials where appropriate.
- **Permissions:** Ensure that uploaded materials and visuals can be shared.
- **Export Quality:** Open the final file and inspect all slides.

Human review is essential because AI-generated content may contain errors, omit context, or introduce unsupported claims.

### Step 6: Export and Deliver the Final Presentation

1. Complete the final quality check.
2. Export the presentation as PDF or PPTX, depending on available options.
3. Alternatively, provide a shareable presentation link.
4. Save the final deliverable in the `outputs/` directory.
5. Save approved infographics in `infographics/`.
6. Add a presentation preview to `screenshots/`.
7. Document the original source links in `sources/source_links.md`.

**Final Deliverable:** A reviewed, reusable training deck for Marketing Intern onboarding.
